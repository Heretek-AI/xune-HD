Xune-HD is a faithful re-creation of the **Microsoft Zune HD on-device
interface** (2009) for modern Android phones: white-on-black typography-first
Metro design, a text menu cropped at the screen edge, Quickplay parked "left
and rear", crossbar navigation, swipe-to-skip Now Playing floating over artist
photography, and the tri-state heart rating.

It is the **device-side sibling** of [Not-Zune](https://github.com/Heretek-AI/not-zune)
(the Zune *desktop* re-implementation). Just as the Zune HD was the device and
Zune 4.8 was the desktop, Xune-HD is the phone and Not-Zune is the desktop.

## ✨ What's implemented

**The full Zune HD on-device experience** (`docs/zune-hd-ui-canon.md`,
`docs/design-tokens.md`, `docs/zcp-inventory.md`):

- **Home menu** — giant lowercase items cropped at the right screen edge:
  `music · videos · pictures · radio · marketplace · social · podcasts ·
  internet · settings` (canon §3.1).
- **Quickplay** — slides out "left and rear" with a 420 ms parallax reveal
  (canon §3.2). Shows Now Playing card, **Pins** (long-press anything to pin),
  **History**, **New**.
- **Music crossbar** — `albums · artists · playlists · songs · genres`, flick
  horizontally to switch pivots.
- **Artist page** — `albums · songs · bio · photos · related` (canon §4):
  bio via Wikipedia REST summary keyed by artist name, photos from
  MusicBrainz, related = same-genre artists from the library.
- **Alphabet rail** with the **full A–Z popup** (canon §3.5).
- **Now Playing** — explicit back arrow, tappable song title → album's
  track list, tappable artist/album, swipe ← → to skip, tap for the
  transport overlay (play/pause center, volume top/bottom, prev/next sides,
  swipe ↑ ↓ for volume), shuffle + repeat + **tri-state heart rating**, idle
  5 s → screensaver with slow-drifting metadata, Ken Burns artist photo or
  Palette-wash backdrop.
- **Tap-the-cut-off-header to go back** — the Zune HD's signature navigation,
  on every screen.
- **Smart DJ shuffle** — hearts prioritized, broken hearts skipped (canon §4).
- **Lock shade** — slide up after the app returns from background (canon §5).
- **Device mode** — the entire UI re-rendered inside a letterboxed 480×272
  canvas, the authentic Zune HD screen.
- **Accent themes** — zune pink, orange, electric cyan, vivid lime, deep
  purple. Palette-driven color wash on Now Playing.

**Media pivots** (canon §3.1):

- **Videos** — MediaStore videos, ExoPlayer playback with surface view +
  custom transport overlay.
- **Pictures** — MediaStore images bucketed by folder, flickable fullscreen
  pager with long-press → pin to Quickplay.
- **Podcasts** — RSS subscription manager (built-in XmlPullParser),
  episodes list, plays through the Media3 session.
- **Radio** — era-faithful FM dial (Canvas + drag), Room-managed stations,
  streams played via Media3. Ships with a few public community-stream
  presets; users can add their own stream URLs.
- **Internet** — a minimal metro-styled WebView with a metro URL bar.
- **Marketplace** — `music · videos · podcasts · apps` crossbar with the
  **Apps pivot** listing every installed mini-app plus the frozen official
  catalog (62 entries from the ZuneRedux archive, all design-reference only).
- **Social** — mock frozen Zune-Social feed (service ended 2012).

**Mini-app platform** (canon §8 — behavioral re-implementations of the
official marketplace apps; no Microsoft code, binaries or assets):

- **Utilities**: calculator (basic + scientific), notes (Room CRUD),
  stopwatch (laps), metronome (PCM synth, BPM / time signature), alarm clock
  (AlarmManager + wake-to-playlist), calendar (month grid + appointments),
  level (accelerometer bubble + flat/surface), piano (multi-touch + sine
  synth), drum machine (16-step sequencer), chord finder (static shapes),
  music quiz (live-library questions), shuffle by album.
- **Games**: solitaire (Klondike), sudoku (backtrack generator), hexic (hex
  cluster rotation), reversi (greedy AI).
- **Offline mock shells**: weather, twitter, facebook, email, messenger,
  msn money, zune reader.

## 📄 Design canon

`docs/zune-hd-ui-canon.md` is the authoritative interaction spec, compiled
from 2009 reviews and community resources. `docs/design-tokens.md` holds the
portable token set. `docs/zcp-inventory.md` lists the 62 official Zune HD
marketplace packages whose metadata was used to design the mini-app
platform — generated by `tools/zcp_inventory.py` from the
ZuneRedux `zune-hd-apps` archive.

## 🛠️ Building

```bash
# Android SDK + JDK 21 required; local.properties points at your SDK
./gradlew assembleDebug          # debug APK
./gradlew test                   # unit tests + design-invariant audit (Robolectric)
./gradlew lint                   # android lint
```

The release build minifies with R8; keep rules in `app/proguard-rules.pro`.

## 🗺️ Roadmap

- Wi-Fi sync with Not-Zune: the phone enrolls as a Zune-HD-like device in
  Not-Zune's sync engine (JSON manifest, ZMDB-style database).
- Real MTP/MTPZ sync to a physical Zune HD over USB host (stretch).
- More games (Hearts, Spades, Chess engine, Checkers, Texas Hold 'Em).
